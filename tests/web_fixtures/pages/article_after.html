City council approves new bike lanes

The city council voted on Thursday to approve forty kilometres of protected bike lanes across the downtown area. The vote was eight to three after a debate that lasted more than two hours.

Supporters said the lanes would reduce traffic deaths, while opponents argued that the plan removes too many parking spaces from small shops. Construction is expected to begin in the autumn and to finish within two years.

The mayor said the budget for the project is twelve million dollars, paid mostly from a regional transport grant. A public report on traffic safety will be published every year.
